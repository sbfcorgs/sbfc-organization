Our Story

Making Every Dollar Count

SBFC Organization was founded in 1998 with a simple but powerful belief: that every person deserves dignity, care, and opportunity — regardless of their economic circumstances.

For over 28 years, we have worked alongside our community, providing emergency medical support, food assistance, and long-term empowerment programs to those who need it most.

Our work is guided by transparency, accountability, and the conviction that collective action creates lasting change.

Full Transparency

Every donation is tracked, verified, and reported. Our balance sheets are available to all stakeholders through our admin portal.

Emergency Response

When a family faces a crisis, we move fast. Funds are verified and released immediately to cover urgent medical and food needs.

100% Volunteer Led

Our organization runs entirely on the dedication of volunteers who believe that serving humanity is its own reward.
