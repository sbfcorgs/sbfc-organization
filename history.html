Our History

From a simple friendship on January 3, 1998, to a movement that has touched thousands of lives across Bangladesh.

The Beginning

A Friendship That
Changed Everything

On January 3, 1998, a simple introduction turned into friendship — and from that friendship, a dream was born: SBFC Organization.

It was built by a few passionate hearts filled with sincerity, love, and a strong desire to do something meaningful for humanity.

Founding Members

  • Md Zahir Uddin Rasel — Founder President
  • Md Mashud Rana — Founder Co-President
  • Md Arif Hossain — Founder
  • Md Jony Hossain — Founder ✦ In Memoriam
  • Md Rubel Hossain — Founder

What started as an ordinary friendship has become a shelter of hope for countless people. SBFC Organization is not just a name — it is a commitment to humanity.

When someone feels lost in despair,
when someone searches for a helping hand,
SBFC Organization becomes that hand of hope.

Every member of this organization does not just work — they serve with their hearts. Their dream is simple: to serve humanity, to stand beside people, and to spread love everywhere.

What began with friendship has grown into a strong foundation of humanity. SBFC Organization is not just an organization — it is a family, a dream, and a symbol of compassion.
